What Colour Is Astro Green Onyx, Really?

Astro Green Onyx is a silver-sage tile with olive and gold accents, not a bright green tile. We measured the colour across four tile faces in the product photos. About 85–90% of each face is a pale grey with a green-sage cast. Soft olive-grey shading covers roughly 9%. The striking dark olive clusters cover only about 1.5%, white crystal flashes about 4%, and gold-ochre veins about 1%.

Those numbers explain the tile's personality. Up close, the deep olive breaks look dramatic and gemstone-like. Across a bathroom, the pale base takes over, and the wall reads as a light, cool, softly green marble. If you have been put off green tiles because they feel heavy, Astro Green is the gentlest way into the colour.

Is Astro Green a bright green tile?

No, Astro Green is a muted grey-green, and it is one of the least saturated green onyx tiles at Tiles Paradise. Tiles such as Onyx Real Green are a true mid-green. Astro Green sits much closer to a sage or eucalyptus shade. Expect "green-tinted marble", not "emerald".

Why does it look cream in some photos and grey-green in others?

Astro Green changes with the colour temperature of the light, because its base is so pale. Warm bulbs (around 2700K) pull the base towards cream and make the gold veins glow. Cool daylight and neutral LED lighting (around 4000K) keep it silver-green. You can see both effects in the room photos on this page. The double-vanity wall further down was shot in warm light, and the shower room at the top in cool light. They are the same tile.

This is the single best reason to order a sample. A cut sample costs £1.99, and a larger 20×50cm sample costs £7.99. Look at it in your own room in the morning and in the evening, under the bulbs you will actually use.

How much does the pattern change from tile to tile?

Astro Green has several different faces, so no two neighbouring tiles need to look the same. The product photos show at least four distinct faces. Some carry heavy olive clusters near one end. Others are calm and cloudy with only thin gold threads.

Dry-lay the tiles on the floor before fixing them. Spread the busiest faces across the wall, rather than letting two dark clusters meet at one joint. On a feature wall, you can do the opposite and place the most dramatic face at eye level, behind a basin or a bath.

Is Astro Green Onyx made of real onyx?

No, Astro Green Onyx is porcelain with a high-definition onyx print under a polished glaze. Real "onyx" sold as slabs is usually a banded form of calcite, often called onyx marble. It is soft, porous, sensitive to acids and needs regular sealing. Some green onyx stone is also dyed. Astro Green gives you the look of a rare gemstone slab, but with the water resistance and low upkeep of porcelain. It also costs a fraction of real green onyx.

Is "Astro" a stone type or a range name?

"Astro" is simply the name of this tile design. It does not describe a mineral, a quarry or a stone grade. So if you search for "Astro green tiles", ignore results for artificial grass or video games. The product you want is the Astro Green Onyx polished porcelain 60×120cm tile.

Is polished Astro Green slippery?

Yes, polished Astro Green is slippery when wet, like every high-gloss porcelain tile. It is safe and comfortable underfoot when dry. Tiles Paradise does not publish a slip rating for this tile. Polished porcelain generally sits at the low end of slip testing. UK guidance treats a wet pendulum test value (PTV) of 36 or more as low slip potential, and mirror-polished surfaces rarely reach that when wet.

Three simple habits make an Astro Green bathroom floor practical:

  • Fit a shower tray or a fully enclosed shower, so water stays off the polished floor.
  • Keep an absorbent bath mat where you step out of the shower or bath.
  • Avoid oily or waxy cleaners, which make the gloss even slicker.

How Do You Fit 60×120cm Polished Porcelain Like Astro Green?

Astro Green should be fitted by someone experienced with large-format tiles, because a 1.2-metre tile shows every uneven surface. The checklist below covers the points that matter most for this tile. Share it with your fitter before the job starts.

  1. Check the wall background. Astro Green weighs around 18kg per m², or roughly 22–25kg per m² with adhesive. UK fitters work to the BS 5385-1 limits of 20kg per m² on gypsum plaster and 32kg per m² on plasterboard. So skimmed plaster is not strong enough. Use plasterboard, cement board or a tile backer board instead.
  2. Make it flat. Floors and walls must be flat and level, because long tiles exaggerate dips. A self-levelling compound fixes uneven floors.
  3. Use the right adhesive. Porcelain needs a flexible cement-based adhesive (S1). Large tiles need a solid bed with no hollow spots, so fitters comb the adhesive with a large notched trowel and back-butter each tile. The tile adhesive and grout range includes Mapei products that suit large porcelain.
  4. Use levelling clips. A levelling clip system keeps neighbouring tile edges flush and stops lippage, which shows clearly on a gloss surface.
  5. Limit the offset. If you stagger the tiles, offset each row by no more than a third of the tile length. A half-offset can make slight bowing in long tiles more visible.
  6. Keep joints slim. The rectified edges allow a 2mm joint on walls. On floors, many fitters widen joints to 3mm to absorb small movement.
  7. Cut with care. Use a rail cutter or wet saw that can take at least a 1,200mm cut, and a fresh blade. Masking tape along the cut line helps protect the polished glaze from chipping.
  8. Leave movement gaps. Leave a gap at the floor perimeter and seal internal corners with flexible silicone, not grout.
  9. Treat underfloor heating gently. Let the adhesive cure fully, as its maker specifies, then bring the heating up gradually the first time.

Our step-by-step guide on laying large-format porcelain floor tiles over concrete and underfloor heating goes into each stage in more detail. Our explainer on rectified porcelain tiles and edge tolerance shows why a 2mm joint works on Astro Green.

Should Astro Green tiles be laid portrait or landscape?

Both work, and the choice changes the feel of the room. Portrait (vertical) tiles make a ceiling feel higher. Two stacked portrait tiles reach 2.4 metres plus joints, which is close to a typical UK ceiling height, so plan the cut row carefully. Landscape (horizontal) tiles make a narrow room feel wider and let the diagonal veining flow sideways.

How Do You Clean and Care for Astro Green Onyx?

Astro Green needs only a soft cloth and a pH-neutral cleaner. Wipe walls with a damp microfibre cloth, then buff dry to stop water spots on the gloss. Mop floors with warm water and a neutral floor cleaner. Avoid abrasive pads, scouring powders and wax- or oil-based polishes, which can dull or smear the polish.

The pale base hides water marks and dust far better than dark polished tiles, so Astro Green looks clean for longer than black or deep green gloss. Grit is the main enemy of any polished floor, so use doormats at external doors. For grout lines and limescale, our guide to cleaning bathroom tiles and grout covers safe products and methods. Do Astro Green tiles need sealing?

No, Tiles Paradise's guidance is that this tile does not need sealing; only the grout benefits from a grout sealer. Some retailers say polished porcelain must be sealed. That advice applies mainly to unglazed, through-body porcelain, where polishing can open tiny surface pores. If you want reassurance, put a drop of oil or a felt-tip mark on a spare off-cut. If it wipes away cleanly after a few minutes, the surface does not need sealing.

Questions Buyers Ask About Astro Green Onyx

Is Astro Green Onyx suitable for underfloor heating?

Yes, Astro Green is suitable for electric and wet underfloor heating. Use a flexible S1 adhesive, let it cure fully, and raise the temperature slowly the first time you switch the system on.

Does polished Astro Green scratch?

Astro Green resists everyday wear, but grit dragged under shoes or furniture can dull any polished glaze over time. Use doormats, felt pads under chair and table legs, and lift furniture rather than dragging it.

Can Astro Green Onyx be used outside?

No, Astro Green is an indoor tile. Polished porcelain is too slippery for outdoor paving. For patios, look at the 60×120cm 20mm outdoor porcelain tiles instead.

Will Astro Green look dated in a few years?

Astro Green is less likely to date than a bright green tile, because its base is a near-neutral grey-sage. Bold fittings and paint colours date faster than the tile itself, and they are much easier to change.

Does Astro Green show water spots and smears?

Light water spots can show on any gloss tile, but Astro Green's pale, busy pattern hides them better than dark polished tiles. A quick buff with a dry microfibre cloth after showering keeps the wall spotless.
