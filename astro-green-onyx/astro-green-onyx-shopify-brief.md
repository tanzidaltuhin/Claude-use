## B. Decision: new article or edit an existing one?

**New article.** No live post covers Astro Green Onyx.

- The SERP script's duplicate check flagged nine posts at 60%. That was only because of the generic words "onyx / polished / porcelain". Every one of them is about a different colourway (Crystal Onyx White, Onyx White, Onyx Cream, Onyx Diora Gold, Venetia Onyx White, Nebula Black and so on).
- The closest live post is `onyx-green-marble-effect-porcelain-tile-guide`. It covers a **different product**: Onyx Green Marble Effect, an aqua-teal tile with rust veins, priced at £25.99. Rewriting it for Astro Green would break that page's entity focus, and it already earns clicks (7 clicks, 275 impressions, pos 8.74).
- So Astro Green gets its own sibling page. The two articles are told apart by colour entity (silver-sage vs aqua-teal), and each links to the other with a clear "not the same tile" statement.

## H. Data sources and accuracy notes

- **Price, specs, samples, delivery, returns, reviews:** live product page, 27 October 2026. Price £24.99/m², RRP £59.99, 8.5 mm, rectified, UFH yes, box 2, weight 13 kg, 5.0 from 10 reviews, cut sample £1.99, 20×50 cm sample £7.99.
- **Weight:** the page lists "13KG". At 8.5 mm and 0.72 m², that figure matches one tile (a B&Q listing gives 27.5 kg for a 2-tile 8 mm pack). The article uses "about 18 kg per m² of tile" rather than a per-box weight.
- **Slip rating:** not published for Astro Green, so the article does not invent one. It gives the PTV 36 benchmark and the polished-porcelain consensus instead.
- **Colour shares:** measured from four product-photo faces with image quantisation. They are labelled "approximate" everywhere.
- **Competitor prices:** given as a range (£35–£80/m²), without naming or linking the retailers.
- **Sibling prices and thickness:** the six product pages, 27 October 2026.
- **Re-check prices before publishing** if more than a few days have passed, because sale prices change.
