What is Crystal Onyx White tile made of?

Crystal Onyx White is made of porcelain, not stone. Porcelain is a dense clay body pressed and fired at a high temperature, so it absorbs very little water. The onyx pattern is printed onto the face, and the surface is then polished to a mirror-like shine.

The name borrows from natural onyx, the translucent, banded stone the tile trade often calls onyx marble. "Crystal" describes the clear, glassy whiteness of the design. The tile copies that look, but it has none of real onyx's softness, so it does not need the careful handling and sealing that a stone floor needs. If you are weighing the real thing against porcelain, our porcelain vs real marble comparison covers cost, etching and upkeep.

Can Crystal Onyx White be backlit like real onyx?

No, Crystal Onyx White cannot be backlit. Porcelain is opaque, so light does not pass through it. Backlit onyx walls and bar fronts use thin slabs of natural stone. The porcelain tile gives the onyx look on a wall or floor at a fraction of the cost, without the glow.

Yes, Crystal Onyx White can be slippery when wet, like any polished porcelain. The listing gives no R or pendulum slip rating, so it should not be used where water pools. The same polished tile on the shower walls, with a matt tile on the shower floor, keeps the look and the grip.

How do you fit 60×120cm polished porcelain like Crystal Onyx White?

Fit Crystal Onyx White on a flat, rigid, dry base, with full adhesive cover and levelling clips. Large polished tiles show every height difference between edges, because the shine catches the light along any step.

  • Check flatness first. Floors and walls must be flat across the full 1.2m length of the tile. Level any dips before you start.
  • Back-butter every tile. Spread adhesive on the back as well as the base, so there are no hollow corners to crack later.
  • Use levelling clips. They hold neighbouring edges at the same height while the adhesive sets.
  • Keep offsets to one-third. A half-brick offset on 60×120cm tiles exaggerates any slight bow in the tile.
  • Plan the cuts. A 120cm tile needs a long rail cutter or a wet saw, and two people to carry and place it.
  • Protect the polish. Keep tape and grout off the face, and clean grout haze within the time the haze remover allows.

Rectified edges let you use a slim 2mm joint on walls for a near-seamless look; 2–3mm is common on floors. The guide to rectified porcelain tiles explains why the edges matter. For the full bathroom sequence, from tanking to silicone, follow how to tile a bathroom step by step.

What grout colour goes with Crystal Onyx White?

White or a light silver-grey grout suits Crystal Onyx White best. White grout makes the joints disappear into the tile. Light grey picks up the veining and hides everyday marks on a floor. Avoid dark grout: it draws a hard grid across a soft onyx pattern. The guide on how to grout tiles covers mixing and finishing narrow joints.

How do you clean Crystal Onyx White without streaks or scratches?

Clean Crystal Onyx White with warm water, a pH-neutral cleaner and a microfibre mop, then buff it dry. Streaks on polished porcelain usually come from too much cleaner or from letting the water dry on the surface.

  • Avoid: wax, oil-based soaps, bleach-heavy sprays and abrasive pads. They dull or film the polish.
  • Scratches: grit carried in on shoes causes most fine marks on polished floors. Use doormats and felt pads under chair legs.
  • Sealing: porcelain does not need routine sealing. If the tile pack advises a pre-grout sealer for polished porcelain, apply it before grouting to stop grout staining the face.
  • Footprints and water marks: they show more on a polished floor than on matt. A quick dry-buff removes them.

Crystal Onyx White questions, answered

Is Crystal Onyx White real onyx?

No. Crystal Onyx White is porcelain with a printed and polished onyx-effect face. It contains no natural stone.

Is Crystal Onyx White polished or matt?

Crystal Onyx White is polished, with a high-gloss face. For a matt white onyx look, choose a matt or satin tile from the same colour family.

How many Crystal Onyx White tiles are in one square metre?

Can you use Crystal Onyx White on a shower floor?

No. Use it on the shower walls, and choose a matt tile with a stated R11 rating for the shower floor.

Can Crystal Onyx White be used outside?

No. It is an indoor polished tile. Patios need 20mm outdoor porcelain with an R11 rating.

Is Crystal Onyx White suitable for a kitchen worktop?

No. It is a wall and floor tile, not a worktop surface. It works well as a splashback behind a worktop.

Do polished porcelain tiles like Crystal Onyx White scratch easily?

Polished porcelain is hard-wearing, but grit can leave fine marks on the shiny face over time. Doormats, felt pads and regular sweeping keep the polish looking new.

Can you tile Crystal Onyx White onto plasterboard?

Yes, if the plasterboard is sound and the total weight of tile plus adhesive stays within the board's limit, commonly quoted as 32kg per m². In wet areas, tank the board first.

Can Crystal Onyx White be laid over existing tiles?

Often, yes, if the old tiles are firmly bonded, flat and clean. Large polished tiles need a very flat base, so check the old floor with a long straight edge first.

Is Crystal Onyx White good for a small bathroom?

Yes. Its bright white and large format make small rooms look bigger. Keep the shower floor matt and use a bath mat on the polished area.

Does Crystal Onyx White need sealing?

No routine sealing is needed for porcelain. Follow any pre-grout sealing advice on the tile pack to protect the polished face during grouting.
