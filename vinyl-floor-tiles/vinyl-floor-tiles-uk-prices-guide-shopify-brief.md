4. **Numeric layout rules.** Competitors say "stagger the joints". The article gives a 200mm (or 25%) stagger, no offcut under 150mm, a last row of at least 50mm, a 6mm gap and transitions beyond 22.9m, plus a diagram.

7. **Honest limits.** Showers, outdoors, strong sun and the Luxura UFH status are stated plainly, not glossed over.
8. **Lot-number advice.** Check that every carton shares the same item and lot number, and blend 2–3 cartons at a time.
