## 9. Store data issues found (fix to avoid confusing buyers)

1. **`/collections/kitchen-wall-tiles` and `/collections/kitchen-floor-tiles` contain the identical 15 products.** The two pages are near-duplicates, which raises cost of retrieval and confuses buyers. Split them by use (wall-only vs floor-rated) or canonicalise one.
2. **The kitchen-tiles collection includes 20mm outdoor pavers** (Lavezzi, Crown Paver, Belgium Beige). Remove them from the kitchen collection.
3. **"California Brown 60x120cm … Polished"** lists **R11**, which is unusual for a polished face. Verify it, or this may mislead buyers into using a polished tile on a wet floor.
4. **Statuario Carrara White Matt Anti-Slip** lists **R9**, while its description says R10 (seen in an earlier audit too).
5. **Title vs handle mismatches:**
   - "Tanvi Grey Matt Textured" sits at `/fabric-grey-matt-textured-…`.
   - "Ucre Wood Effect" sits at `/ecru-wood-effect-…`; "Ucre" looks like a typo of "Ecru".
6. **The Marena Blue gloss metro** gallery's first image shows teal-green tiles. Check the colour or the image.
7. **Price outliers** listed as standard products in the kitchen collection: an Aurora Onyx Gold pallet (£1,225), a 60 sqm pallet (£1,200) and a Statuario Carrara job lot (£599). They distort "price from/to" sorting.
