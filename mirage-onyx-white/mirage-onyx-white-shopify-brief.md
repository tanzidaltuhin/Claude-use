## H. Accuracy notes (so buyers are never misled)

- Price (£19.99/m², RRP £34.99), sample prices, 9mm, rectified, UFH, 4 per box and delivery and return terms are copied from the live product page on 27 Oct 2026. **Update the article if the price changes.**
- The product page lists **Weight: 6kg** without saying per tile or per box. Calculated weight for a 9mm 60×60 porcelain tile is about 7.5 kg, so the article does not quote the 6kg figure. **Suggest correcting the product page.**
- **No slip (R) rating is published** for Mirage Onyx White. The article says so instead of inventing one. If the supplier sheet gives a figure, add it to the spec table and the slip H3.
- The product page lists "Pattern Repeats: 1", which is ambiguous. The article only states what the photos show: at least four vein faces.
- Competitor prices quoted (about £42–£43/m²) were taken from two UK retailers' 60×60 white onyx-effect pages in October 2026. No competitor is named in the article.
- BS 5385 figures are given as UK guidance values and hedged with "about".
