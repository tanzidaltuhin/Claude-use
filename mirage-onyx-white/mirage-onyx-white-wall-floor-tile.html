Is Mirage Onyx White Made by Mirage, the Italian Tile Brand?

No. Mirage Onyx White is the name of this Tiles Paradise porcelain tile. It is not part of the onyx-look ranges sold by Mirage, the Italian porcelain maker. People often type "Mirage onyx" or "Mirage onyks" into Google and find the Italian brand's products. If you want the 60×60cm polished tile at £19.99 per m², this is the one.

Is it real onyx?

No. Mirage Onyx White is porcelain with a printed onyx pattern under a polished glaze. Natural onyx used in homes is usually "onyx marble", a banded, see-through calcium carbonate stone. That stone is soft, it can etch from lemon juice or vinegar, and it needs sealing. Porcelain gives you the banded look without any of that care.

Why does onyx-effect porcelain cost so much less than the stone?

Porcelain is pressed and fired in large batches, so every tile comes out the same size and thickness. Natural onyx slabs are cut from rare blocks, and a lot of the stone breaks or gets thrown away. That is why the real stone costs far more per m². An onyx-effect tile at £19.99 per m² puts the look within reach of a normal bathroom budget.

What Does Mirage Onyx White Look Like in a Real Room?

Mirage Onyx White looks bright, cool and calm. The base is close to pure white. The veining is soft, wavy and light grey, more like ripples in sand than the sharp lines of a marble tile. There is no gold or beige in the pattern, so it sits well with chrome, brushed nickel, matt black or brass.

The product photos show at least four different vein faces. That variety matters on a floor. If you lay tiles straight from one box, two matching faces can end up side by side. Open three or four boxes and shuffle the tiles before you fix them. Turn some tiles 90° as well, so the bands flow in more than one direction.

How does onyx effect differ from marble effect?

Onyx effect shows soft, layered bands, like rings in a cut stone. Marble effect shows crisp, branching veins on a plain ground. Mirage Onyx White reads as gentle movement across a white surface, so it looks quieter than a bold Calacatta-style marble tile.

Will the white look cold?

It can in a north-facing room under cool LED bulbs. Warm white bulbs (around 2700K to 3000K), timber and soft fabrics take the edge off. In a sunny room the polish bounces daylight around and makes the space feel bigger. That is the main reason people choose a polished white tile for a small bathroom.

Is Mirage Onyx White slippery?

Yes, when it is wet. Any polished tile loses grip once water, soap or oil sits on the surface. The product page lists no R rating, so treat it like other polished porcelain, which usually tests at R9 or below. In UK slip testing, a wet Pendulum Test Value under 36 points to a higher slip risk, and wet polished porcelain usually scores well below that. Dry, the tile is fine for normal walking.

Why Mirage Onyx White Comes in 60×60cm Only, and Why That Helps

Mirage Onyx White is made in one size: 60×60cm. Some buyers expect a large 60×120cm slab for a polished stone look. The square format has real advantages, though.

  • Easier handling: one person can lift and place a 60×60cm tile. A 60×120cm slab usually needs two people and suction lifters.
  • Less lippage: a smaller tile bows less, so the edges sit level more easily. That matters on a polished floor, where light shows every raised edge.
  • Less waste in small rooms: en-suites, cloakrooms and narrow hallways need many cuts. Smaller tiles waste less at each wall.
  • Works on walls: a 60×60cm tile is easier to set on a vertical wall than a long slab.
  • Clear scale: a 3×3 grid of tiles fills a typical bath panel wall neatly.

Fitting Mirage Onyx White on Walls

Mirage Onyx White can go on most sound, flat walls, but the backing decides how safe the job is. A 9mm porcelain tile weighs roughly 21 kg per m², and the adhesive adds a few more kilos. UK tiling guidance (BS 5385-1) puts the limit at about 20 kg per m² on gypsum plaster and about 32 kg per m² on plasterboard without a skim coat.

  • Plastered wall: tile and adhesive together can go over the 20 kg per m² limit. Most fitters take the plaster off, or line the wall with board.
  • Plasterboard: fine within the 32 kg per m² limit, as long as the board is fixed firmly. Prime it first if the adhesive maker says so.
  • Shower walls: use cement backer board or a tanking membrane, so water never reaches the board behind.
  • Old tiles: tiling over tiles adds weight and can trap loose tiles. Strip them unless you are sure every tile is bonded.

Rectified edges let you use a slim 2mm joint on walls, which gives a near-seamless, stone-panel look. Our guide to what rectified porcelain tiles are explains why this only works on a truly flat wall.

Fitting Mirage Onyx White on Floors

A polished floor shows every raised edge, because the light catches it. A flat base, full adhesive cover and a sensible layout are what keep the gloss looking smooth.

  1. Check the floor is flat. UK guidance (BS 5385-3) allows about 3mm of dip under a 2-metre straight edge. Fill hollows with levelling compound before you tile.
  2. Prepare timber floors. Floorboards move. Screw down cement backer board or a decoupling mat first, so the tiles do not crack.
  3. Choose a straight grid or a one-third offset. A half offset (brick pattern) puts the high point of one tile next to the low edge of the next, so lippage shows most.
  4. Use a 10–12mm notched trowel. Spread adhesive on the floor and a thin coat on the back of each tile. Aim for a solid bed with no hollow spots.
  5. Fit levelling clips. They pull neighbouring edges level while the adhesive sets.
  6. Leave a 3mm joint on floors. The tile allows 2mm, but 3mm gives more room for movement, especially over underfloor heating.
  7. Leave a gap at the walls. Fill the perimeter with flexible silicone, not grout. Large floors also need movement joints across the room.
  8. Bring underfloor heating up slowly. Wait for the adhesive and grout to cure as the maker states. Then raise the temperature a little each day.
  9. Protect the polish during work. Cover the finished floor with breathable board or card while other trades are on site.

How do you cut and drill a 9mm polished porcelain tile?

A manual rail cutter scores and snaps straight cuts in 9mm porcelain cleanly. Use a wet saw or an angle grinder with a diamond blade for L-shapes and notches. Drill holes with a diamond core bit, hammer action off, and keep the bit cool with water. Put masking tape over the polished face to stop the bit wandering.

Which Grout Colour Suits Mirage Onyx White?

White or a very light grey grout suits Mirage Onyx White best. White joints disappear into the white base and make the surface look like one stone panel. A silver-grey joint picks up the grey veining and frames each tile gently. Mapei Ultracolor Plus 100 White and 111 Silver Grey are two common choices.

Avoid charcoal or black grout on a polished white tile. Dark pigment can catch in the fine surface of the glaze during grouting and leave a grey haze. If you really want a dark joint, test it on a spare tile first.

Clear the grout haze off the polish within the time the grout maker gives, using a damp sponge and a clean rinse. A grout sealer, applied once the grout has cured, keeps light joints from browning in kitchens and showers. Our step-by-step grouting guide covers mixing, timing and clean-up. Keeping a Polished White Floor Looking New

Mirage Onyx White is easy to clean. Porcelain absorbs almost no water, so spills sit on top and wipe away. The real enemy of a polished floor is grit, because sand is harder than the glaze and can leave fine scratches.

  • Daily: sweep or vacuum with a soft brush head to lift grit.
  • Weekly: mop with warm water and a pH-neutral floor cleaner, then dry with a microfibre mop to stop water spots.
  • Avoid: wax, polish-boosters and oil soaps, which leave a slippery, cloudy film. Avoid scouring pads and strong acids on the grout.
  • Protect: put felt pads under chair and table legs, and fit a doormat at every outside door.

Our guide to cleaning bathroom tiles and grout covers limescale and mould on shower walls.

Does a white polished floor show dirt?

It shows dark crumbs, hair and muddy footprints more than a mid-tone tile does. It hides dust, water marks and limescale better than a black or dark tile. The soft grey veining also helps break up small marks.

Will Mirage Onyx White turn yellow?

No. The colour is fired into a porcelain glaze, so sunlight and cleaning products do not yellow it. When a white tiled floor starts to look dull or yellow, the cause is usually cleaner residue or dirty grout, not the tile.
