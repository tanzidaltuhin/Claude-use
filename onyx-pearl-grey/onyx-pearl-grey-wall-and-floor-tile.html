What Colour Is Onyx Pearl Grey in Real Life?

Onyx Pearl Grey is a very light, slightly warm grey that sits close to white. We measured the average colour of all four product photographs. The faces average #EDEBE7 to #F1EFEB, a neutral off-white with a whisper of warmth. The grey lives in the cloudy drifts and the thin silver veins, not in the base colour.

"Pearl grey" describes that effect. The name points to the pale, lustrous grey on the inside of a pearl shell. It is a highlight colour, not a shadow colour. Buyers expecting a concrete or dove grey are the ones most likely to be surprised.

Light changes the tile more than most. A north-facing bathroom will push it towards cool white. Warm 2700K downlights will pull out its slight cream undertone. The polished face also mirrors whatever is opposite it, so a navy vanity or a wooden door will tint the floor in front of it.

The tile comes with multiple pattern faces. Each face carries its own vein: one diagonal, one soft and horizontal, one scattered, one crossing. Lay them straight out of one box and the same face can land side by side. Open two or three boxes, lay the tiles out dry, and shuffle until no two matching veins meet.

Is Onyx Pearl Grey Real Onyx?

No. Onyx Pearl Grey is porcelain carrying a printed onyx design, not a cut slab of stone. That difference is the reason it suits a family bathroom.

Most decorative "onyx" sold for interiors is banded calcite, sometimes called onyx marble. It is translucent, soft (around 3 on the Mohs scale) and etched by acids such as lemon juice, vinegar and some limescale removers. It needs sealing and careful cleaning. Real onyx slabs also cost many times more per square metre.

Porcelain is a dense, fired ceramic. Porcelain tiles in Group BIa of BS EN 14411 absorb 0.5% water or less. So the tile face takes splashes, shampoo and cleaning sprays without staining or etching. What you give up is translucency: Onyx Pearl Grey cannot be backlit the way a real onyx panel can.

Where Can You Use Onyx Pearl Grey?

Use Onyx Pearl Grey on any indoor wall and on indoor floors that stay dry. The polished face loses grip when water, soap or oil sits on it. The product page does not publish an R-rating, and our own fitting advice says a matt or anti-slip finish is safer underfoot in showers and wet rooms.

Can Onyx Pearl Grey go on a bathroom floor?

Yes, if the shower sits in a tray or an enclosure. Water then lands on the tray, not on the tiles. Keep a bath mat by the bath and wipe up splashes straight away. In a 60×120 format, a typical 2×2m bathroom floor needs only six to eight full tiles, so the room looks larger and has fewer joints to clean. Can it be used on a walk-in shower or wet-room floor?

No. A flush wet-room floor stays wet for most of the day, and a high-gloss tile is the wrong surface for bare, soapy feet. The answer is a two-tile plan. Keep Onyx Pearl Grey on the shower walls and lay a matt anti-slip grey floor in the same 60×120 size. Is it a good kitchen or hallway floor?

It can be, with habits to match. Kitchens bring oil and water. Hallways bring rain and grit on shoes. Put a doormat inside the front door and a washable runner by the sink. Can Onyx Pearl Grey be used outside?

No. It is sold for interior walls and floors. A polished face on a patio becomes dangerous in rain and frost, and outdoor porcelain is normally 20mm thick and textured.

How Do You Fit 60×120cm Polished Porcelain Like Onyx Pearl Grey?

Fit Onyx Pearl Grey on a flat, sound surface, in a solid bed of flexible S1 adhesive, with 2mm joints, levelling clips and an offset of no more than one-third. A 1.2-metre polished tile shows every uneven edge, because the gloss catches side light like a mirror. Listed below are the steps a fitter should follow.

  1. Check flatness. Aim for no more than a 3mm gap under a 2m straightedge (SR1 under BS 8204). Fix high spots and fill dips before tiling.
  2. Check the wall can carry the weight. At about 18kg per m² of tile, plus adhesive, the finished wall weighs roughly 23–25kg per m². That sits inside the 32kg per m² commonly given for tiling direct to plasterboard, but above the 20kg per m² limit for a gypsum plaster skim. Over plaster, fit a cement backer board first.
  3. Use the right adhesive. Choose a cement-based C2 S1 flexible adhesive (BS EN 12004), such as the Mapei Keraflex Maxi S1 in our tile adhesive range.
  4. Get full coverage. Comb the adhesive with a 10–12mm notched trowel and back-butter each tile. A hollow corner on a long polished tile can crack under a chair leg.
  5. Keep joints slim and level. The rectified edges allow 2mm joints. Many fitters use 3mm on heated floors. Put levelling clips on every joint.
  6. Offset by one-third. Large tiles carry a slight factory bow. A half-bond offset puts the raised centre of one tile beside the lower end of the next.
  7. Handle in pairs. Each tile weighs about 13kg and flexes when carried flat. Carry it on edge, with two people for floors.
  8. Cut with the right kit. Use a rail cutter at least 1250mm long or a wet saw with a continuous-rim porcelain blade.
  9. Leave movement joints. Leave a gap at walls and other perimeter edges, and fill it with flexible silicone rather than grout.

Rectified tiles are what make the near-seamless look possible. Our explainer on rectified porcelain tiles shows why their edges allow such slim joints.

Does Onyx Pearl Grey work with underfloor heating?

Yes. The tile is listed as suitable for underfloor heating, and porcelain passes heat through well. Fix it with a flexible S1 adhesive. Let the adhesive cure for the time on the bag, often around seven days, before the first heat-up. Then raise the temperature slowly, a couple of degrees a day. How Do You Clean and Protect a Polished Pearl-Grey Floor?

Clean Onyx Pearl Grey with warm water and a pH-neutral tile cleaner, then buff it dry with a microfibre cloth. Drying matters on a gloss face, because water left to dry leaves spots and streaks.

  • Sweep or vacuum first. Grit under a shoe acts like sandpaper on a polished surface.
  • Avoid wax, oil soaps and "shine" polishes. They leave a film that turns dull and slippery.
  • Keep strong acid cleaners off the grout lines. Acids eat cement grout even though the porcelain face resists them.
  • Fit felt pads under chair and table legs.
  • Put doormats at outside doors to stop grit reaching the floor.

The tile face does not need sealing. The grout does: a grout sealer keeps silver-grey grout from darkening in kitchens and showers. Our bathroom tile and grout cleaning guide covers the routine week by week.

Questions Buyers Ask About Onyx Pearl Grey

Is Onyx Pearl Grey grey or white?

It is a pale grey that looks almost white from a distance. The grey shows in its soft clouds and silver veins.

Is Onyx Pearl Grey slippery?

It is slippery when wet, like any high-gloss porcelain. It is fine on dry floors with a mat near water, but not on shower or wet-room floors.

Can I put Onyx Pearl Grey on shower walls?

Yes. Fix it over a tanked board or a waterproofed wall, seal the grout, and use silicone in the internal corners.

How many Onyx Pearl Grey tiles are in a box?

Each box holds 2 tiles, which cover 1.44m². One square metre takes 1.39 tiles.

How heavy is a 60×120 Onyx Pearl Grey tile?

Can Onyx Pearl Grey go onto plasterboard?

Yes, onto sound, well-fixed plasterboard that is primed to the adhesive maker's instructions. Do not tile it onto a gypsum plaster skim, which carries only about 20kg per m².

Can I lay Onyx Pearl Grey over existing tiles?

Only if the old tiles are firmly bonded, flat and able to take the extra weight, and the added height suits your doors. Our guide to tiling over tile lists the checks.

What colour grout goes with Onyx Pearl Grey?

Silver grey or light grey grout gives the most seamless look. White grout makes the tile edges stand out.

Does polished porcelain scratch?

The porcelain body is hard, but grit dragged across a gloss face can leave fine marks. Doormats, felt pads and regular sweeping prevent them.

Is Onyx Pearl Grey good for a small bathroom?

Yes. The pale colour and the shine bounce light, and the large format means fewer grout lines. Run the tiles floor to ceiling to make the room feel taller.

Will the tiles match the photos?

Screens show colour differently, and real tiles vary slightly by batch. Order a sample and buy the full amount in one order.

Does Onyx Pearl Grey need sealing?

The tile face does not. Seal the grout lines to keep them clean.
